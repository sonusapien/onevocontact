QUESTIONS

Straight answers

What is ONEVO?

+

ONEVO is a modern Android application designed to make digital identity and contact sharing simpler, faster, and more convenient. It lets you create a unified digital profile, share it via QR codes, and connect with peers through nearby sharing and proximity interactions.

How does ONEVO work?

+

After installing ONEVO, you set up your profile with the contact methods you wish to make available (such as phone numbers, emails, and social handles). You can then share your profile instantly using a dynamic QR code, broadcast via ONEVO Nearby, or initiate a proximity-based connection.

How do I share my ONEVO profile?

+

You can share your profile in three ways: by displaying your personal ONEVO QR code for the other person to scan with their camera, by initiating an exchange with users discovered in ONEVO Nearby, or by holding compatible phones together with ONEVO NameLink.

Can someone scan my QR code without ONEVO?

+

Yes. When someone scans your ONEVO QR code using a standard smartphone camera or QR scanner, it opens your compatible contact card (standard vCard/web profile) so they can save your details directly into their device's address book without needing the app installed. Fellow ONEVO users unlock a direct mutual save inside the app.

What is ONEVO Nearby?

+

ONEVO Nearby is a local contact discovery feature that lets you find other ONEVO users in your immediate surroundings (for example, in a meeting room or networking hall) and exchange profile cards directly upon mutual approval. It is built specifically for contact cards and does not perform arbitrary file transfers.

What is ONEVO NameLink?

+

ONEVO NameLink is our signature proximity-inspired contact-sharing feature. By bringing two supported Android phones close together, NameLink detects the nearby interaction and initiates a seamless, elegant profile exchange with visual confirmation.

Does NameLink work on every Android phone?

+

Automatic proximity detection requires compatible device hardware and sensors. On devices without dedicated proximity hardware, manual ONEVO Nearby and QR sharing provide dependable, zero-friction alternatives available across all compatible Android phones.

How do I install the APK?

+

Download the APK using the "Download ONEVO" button. Once downloaded, tap the notification or open your Downloads folder to run the APK. If prompted by Android, grant permission to allow installation from your browser. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the installation.

How do I update ONEVO?

+

Whenever a new release is published, download the updated APK directly from this official website and install it. Android will update the existing ONEVO application seamlessly while preserving your profile and saved contacts.

Where can I contact ONEVO support?

+

You can reach the official ONEVO support team via email at support@onevo.app. Our team is available to assist with inquiries, bug reports, and feedback.

ONEVO Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Official Public Launch 2026

At ONEVO, your digital identity belongs to you. We design our contact-sharing tools to give you clear, transparent control over what information you choose to make available.

1. Information You Choose to Share

ONEVO only transmits contact fields (such as phone numbers, email addresses, or profile handles) that you explicitly enable. You can inspect your active card before initiating any exchange.

2. Local Discovery & Exchange

Nearby discovery and NameLink interactions require explicit confirmation on your device before contact information is transferred or stored. No unsolicited contact cards are pushed to your device without approval.

3. Device Storage & Address Book

Contacts received through ONEVO can be managed within the app or exported directly to your native Android Contacts provider at your discretion.

4. Contact Support

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or your profile data, contact us at support@onevo.app.
